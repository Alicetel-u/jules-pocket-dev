## Design Notes
- Base: Dark navy (#0f172a).
- Symbol: A pocket shape representing "Pocket Dev" (#1e293b, #6366f1).
- AI Core: An inner shining core symbol representing AI (#a5b4fc, #e0e7ff).
- Connection: Circuits/lines extending from the core to represent digital development and connectivity (#818cf8).
- Minimalist and scalable: Designed to be easily recognizable at small sizes, with a single central motif, avoiding text.
